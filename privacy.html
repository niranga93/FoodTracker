Nourio Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 25, 2026 · Author: NNRZONE (@Niranga)

Summary

Nourio keeps your food log, profile and settings on your phone, and it has no accounts, ads or analytics. The app is run by NNRZONE ("we"). It sends data off your phone only for features you use: meal analysis, the AI coach and Nourio Assistant (the AI service you choose, using your own API key), recipe links you share (fetched from that website), food lookups and product searches (USDA FoodData Central and Open Food Facts), optional backup to your own Google Drive, and a monthly check with your AI service for changed health guidelines (only the guideline values are sent). If you turn them on, it also reads from and writes to Health Connect on your phone.

Data Stored on Your Phone

Your food log and settings are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone, and we cannot see them. This includes:

  • Meals, with their foods, amounts, nutrition values and times
  • Photos you take of meals, kept in the app's temporary cache folder until Android clears it or you clear the app's data
  • Profile details you enter: name, year of birth, sex, height and weight
  • Goals, water intake, weight entries (including any imported from Health Connect), and favorite foods and meals
  • Diet and allergy choices, and foods whose nutrition match you corrected
  • Your Gemini API key, encrypted with a key held in Android's secure key store
  • Cached nutrition lookups, your Health Connect choices, and a list of the record IDs the app has synced with Health Connect
  • Your latest Insights tips, which tips you accepted or dismissed, and your sleep goal

These services also see your IP address, as any website does. Requests to Gemini are made directly from your phone with the Gemini API key you add, so Google handles them under the terms that apply to your key. Barcode scanning runs on your phone, and the camera image is not uploaded for it. If you speak a meal description, Android's speech recognition service turns it into text under its own privacy terms, and the app receives only that text.

Health Connect Data

Nourio uses Health Connect only for the parts you turn on and allow, and it uses the data only on your phone to show it to you.

Access Data How it is used
Read (Health Connect switch) Steps, active calories burned, total calories burned Show your activity and estimate calories burned and your energy balance
Read (Read sleep, heart rate, workouts, weight) Sleep sessions (including sleep stages), heart rate, exercise sessions, weight. If you also allow them: blood pressure, distance, height Show sleep, heart rate, workouts and distance on the Burned page. Distance makes the calories-burned estimate more accurate. Weights from other apps and smart scales are added to your weigh-ins and update your profile weight. Height fills your profile only if you have not entered one. Sleep, heart rate, blood pressure and steps are also compared with your food log on the Insights page (see below).
Write (Share meals, water, weight) Nutrition (each meal's name, calories and nutrients), hydration (daily water total), weight Make your log available to other health apps you choose. Edits and deletions in Nourio are copied to Health Connect.

Sleep, heart rate, workout and distance data is read when you open the Burned page or calculate calories burned, and is not stored by the app. Health Connect data is not sent to Gemini or any other service, is not shared with third parties, and is not used for advertising. The one exception is a health summary PDF you make yourself (Profile, then Data & privacy): it includes your average steps, calories burned and sleep for the last 30 days, and goes only where you save or send it. Steps, calories, sleep, heart rate, workouts and distance are not included in your Google Drive backup. A weight imported from Health Connect becomes one of your weigh-ins, and an imported height becomes part of your profile. If you turn on Google Drive backup, they are saved in your own Drive with your other weigh-ins and profile.

You can turn off each part at any time in the app (Profile, then Connections) or remove the app's permissions in Health Connect settings. After you turn reading off, the app stops reading and hides this data. Weigh-ins already imported stay until you delete them. After you turn writing off, the app stops writing, and records it already wrote stay in Health Connect until you delete them there.

Insights, Patterns and Daily Tips

The Insights page compares your last 60 days of meals, water, weigh-ins, steps, sleep, heart rate and blood pressure to find patterns (for example, whether you eat more after short nights) and suggests tips. This analysis runs entirely on your phone. None of this data is sent to Gemini, to us or to anyone else.

If you allow notifications, the app shows one tip a day. Accepting a tip changes the matching goal in the app (such as your sodium limit or step goal), and you can change it back at any time in Goals & target. You can turn daily tips off on the Insights page. Patterns show what tends to happen together in your data, not proof that one thing causes another, and tips are general wellness guidance, not medical advice.

AI Features and Your AI Service

You choose one AI service under Profile, then Connections, and add your own API key for it. Requests go straight from your phone to that company; Nourio has no server in between. Only one service is used at a time, and switching to another turns off every AI consent so each feature asks again before sending anything to the new company. If you choose OpenRouter, it passes requests on to the company behind the model you pick. Each request contains only what is listed here:

  • Photo analysis: a resized JPEG of the meal and fixed instructions. It includes no name, profile or health data.
  • Text analysis: the meal you type or speak, or the text you share to the app, and fixed instructions. For a shared link, this is the recipe text read from that page (at most about 6,000 characters).
  • AI coach: the selected day's foods, amounts and totals; your daily maximums if you set them; water intake; an estimate of calories burned that your phone calculates from your profile; and your diet and allergy choices.
  • Nourio Assistant: the question you type, your earlier questions in the same chat with the plans Gemini wrote for them, today's date, and a fixed list of the kinds of data the app keeps (for example "protein per day" or "hours asleep"). Gemini replies with a plan: which records to look at, over which dates, and how to add them up, plus a sentence with blanks. Your phone runs that plan on your own data and fills in the blanks. None of your meals, weigh-ins, Health Connect data, goals or results are sent, and the answers are not sent back to Gemini. You can also ask the assistant to delete data, withdraw a consent, or report a bug, a feature idea or a problem with your data. Gemini only suggests the action; the app shows exactly what will happen, and nothing changes until you confirm it on your phone. A report opens your own email app addressed to us, with the text for you to edit; it is sent only if you press send there, and it contains your logged data only if you add it yourself. You can choose to include the app version and phone model.
  • Guideline updates: once a month, the health guideline values the app uses (such as the 2,300 mg sodium limit) and the guideline each comes from. With Gemini or Claude, the AI service may search the web to check official sources. Nothing about you is sent. Any change it reports is shown to you with the current and the new value and its source, and nothing changes unless you approve it.
  • Never sent: your name, year of birth, sex, height, weight, Health Connect data, or your Google account.

Each of these five features (meal photo analysis, meal descriptions, the AI coach, Nourio Assistant and guideline updates) asks for your consent before it first sends anything, and tells you exactly what it sends. When it asks, you can choose "Always allow" to give consent for all five at once, so you aren't asked again. You can turn each one, or "Always allow AI features", on or off at any time under Profile, then Data & privacy, in "AI and your data"; while one is off, it sends nothing. Photos queued while offline are only analyzed if meal photo analysis is on. If analyzing a meal photo or description takes longer than 30 seconds, the app finishes it in the background and adds the meal to your log; it is sent only while the matching consent is on, and you can edit or delete the meal afterwards like any other. Saved summaries and the assistant's answers stay on your phone. Gemini features need your own Gemini API key, which you add under Profile, then Connections. Requests go to Google under the terms of your key. On the free tier, Google may use requests and responses to improve its products, and human reviewers may read them. Photograph only food, and share only text you are comfortable sending. Without a key, the Gemini features stay off.

AI summaries and portion estimates can be wrong.

What We Do Not Do

We do not sell or share your data, show ads, or track you.

  • No accounts: you do not sign up with us, and we run no server that stores your data.
  • No advertising or analytics code in the app.
  • No crash-reporting code of our own. Google Play and Android may collect their own diagnostics under Google's policies.

Storage, Security and Retention

Your data stays on your phone until you delete it, and we keep no copy. The app sends requests over HTTPS, and its data sits in private app storage that other apps cannot read on a normal, unrooted phone.

If you use Google Drive backup, the backup file stays in your Google Drive until you delete it. If you use Save file under Profile, then Connections, the app writes a backup file, with the same contents as the Drive backup, to the place you pick (for example your phone, Google Drive or Dropbox). That file is not encrypted, it is handled by the storage service you chose, and it stays there until you delete it. If Android's own device backup is turned on, Google may also include this app's data in your phone backup, except your Gemini keys and the values the app derives from Health Connect (sync records and Insights tips), which are always left out. Records the app writes to Health Connect are kept by Health Connect under its own settings until you delete them there.

Your Choices and Deleting Your Data

You control your data in the app, and you can delete all of it yourself:

  • Delete entries: remove meals, weight entries and favorites inside the app.
  • Export: Profile, then Data & privacy, saves your meals, daily totals and weights as CSV files to a place you choose, and makes a 30-day health summary PDF (nutrition, weight trend, and steps, burn and sleep from Health Connect) that you can save or share, for example by email to your doctor. The PDF is made on your phone; Nourio does not send it anywhere itself, and a shared copy is handled by the app you pick.
  • Health Connect: turn off reading or writing under Profile, then Connections, or remove permissions in Health Connect settings. To delete records Nourio wrote, use Health Connect's data settings.
  • Gemini key: remove it under Profile, then Connections. You can also delete the key in Google AI Studio.
  • Google Drive: disconnect under Profile, then Connections. This stops backups but leaves your Drive copy. To delete it, open Google Drive on the web, go to Settings, then Manage apps, and delete this app's hidden data. You can also remove the app's access in your Google Account settings.
  • Avoid sending data to Gemini: log with barcode scan, manual entry, packaged search or favorites instead of photos, descriptions or sharing, and do not use the AI coach.
  • Delete everything on your phone: clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app.

Children

Nourio is not meant for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect data from children. The app turns off calorie-burn and balance estimates for profiles under 18.

Not Medical Advice

Nourio gives general estimates, not medical advice. Calories burned, balance, weight forecasts and AI summaries are estimates and can be wrong. Allergen and diet warnings are best effort: photos cannot show hidden ingredients, so read labels if you have an allergy. Talk to a doctor or dietitian about health, weight or allergy decisions.
